# Fonts

The live page uses two SIL Open Font License 1.1 families, vendored as WOFF2
(latin + latin-ext subsets, full Slovak incl. ĺ Ĺ ŕ Ŕ) in
`themes/_shared/fonts/` and declared in `themes/_shared/fonts.css`:

| Family | Weights | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Manrope | 500, 600, 700 | kicker (500), clock (600), date, „Dôležité“, °C range, grey all-caps labels (700) |
| Inter | 400, 600 | all other text |

Downloaded from Fontsource (jsDelivr CDN), e.g.
`https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/fontsource/fonts/manrope@latest/latin-ext-700-normal.woff2`.

Archived themes under `themes/archive/` keep their own fonts
(Papier: Source Serif 4 / Source Sans 3, Farbičky: Nunito, Večerná: Fraunces / Atkinson Hyperlegible).
